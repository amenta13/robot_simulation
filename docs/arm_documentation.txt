Documentation of important variables in myBlockly coding.

x:
    moves arm left(+) and right(-)
    Max value: 280
    Min value: -280
y:
    moves arm forward(+) and backwward(-)
    Max value: 280
    Min value: -280
z:
    moves arm up(+) and down(-)
    Max value: 280
    Min value: 0

The tip of the gripper will be at coordinates (x,y,z)


rx: rotates about the X-axis; tilts the wrist forward/backward (like nodding "yes" or "no")
ry: rotates about the Y-axis; tilts the wrist side-to-side (like tilting "maybe")
rz: rotates about the Z-axis; rotates the gripper clockwise/counterclockwise (like turning a doorknob)

Each measured in degrees from -180 to 180


speed: normalized speed; 10 - moves gently and smoothly: 50 - moderate speed; 100 - full speed


J1:
    Base rotation
    Max value: 160
    Min value: -160
J2:
    Shoulder
    Max value: 100
    Min value: -100
J3:
    Elbow
    Max value: 100
    Min value: -100
J4:
    Wrist pitch
    Max value: 180
    Min value: -180
J5:
    Wrist yaw
    Max value: 180
    Min value: -180
J6:
    Wrist roll
    Max value: 180
    Min value: -180

J1-J3 mostly controls position (location of gripper)
J4-J6 mostly controls orientation (angle of gripper)
Setting initial values to (0,0,0,0,0,0) sets arm vertically erect
